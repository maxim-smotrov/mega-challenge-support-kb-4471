Each report is counted once: the export repeats some reports, and the table keeps one per id. Each problem has its own ticket.

The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so a date like 4 July comes out as 7 April. It reaches people through the app, by mail and through booking-v2 forms. This is the bug #4471 names, and the queued fix is for it.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells day-first people to type month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY under it. People type what the hint says, and the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. The month flips again, for a different reason. Only form reports from tenants on booking-v3 have this problem. The parser fix leaves this hint as it is.

one-day-move: the date moves by one day, not by months. These reports describe a Tuesday that shows as Monday, a meeting that two clients in different time zones got on two different days, and an invite that arrived before its booking. Day and month are not swapped. This looks like a time zone shift, not a parse. The desk read these reports as one-day moves (desk notes), but they were filed under parser by tenant and so they vanished into the count.

Why one ticket hid three

  • The desk merges on subject line (decision 0001) and never reads a body. A subject like “Wrong date again” says nothing about the cause: all three problems arrive under the same subjects, and the triage explorer shows same-subject pairs with different causes.
  • Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser. The tag is a guess and often disagrees with the cause: see the “tagged as” column.
  • The cause table on #4471 shows hint as “not this ticket — no ticket of its own”. That was true. It has one now.

What the desk does next

  • Keep #4471 for parser. Do not close the other two when its fix ships.
  • When a merged ticket passes a few dozen reports, read a sample of bodies before calling it known.
